GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Do I need an account?

No. Browse and open games as a guest. Hub accounts are not open yet. Favorites and recently opened games stay in this browser.

Are these the original games?

No. Each card names an independent edition. Every game page links to its source and the corresponding original game.

Are my scores synced?

No. This hub stores only local favorites and recently opened game pages. Game progress belongs to the separate game site.

Can I play on my phone?

The hub is designed for small screens. For more game space, use the independent-open button. Actual touch controls and Safari fullscreen still require device testing.
