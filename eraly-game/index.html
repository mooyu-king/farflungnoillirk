ERALY

What is Eraly?

Eraly is a daily history guessing game built around a simple question: in what year did this happen? Players read an event, enter a date, and discover how close they came. Battles, inventions, discoveries and famous journeys turn a short round into a test of historical intuition. This page offers a playable independent Eraly edition with its own question bank and error-based scoring.

To play Eraly here, choose Daily or Practice above, enter a positive common-era year, confirm it, and read the reveal. Fern reacts to your distance from the answer. The exact date appears beneath the score, alongside a short historical note and a reference link for further reading.

LESS DISTANCE, BETTER RESULT

How does Eraly scoring work here?

Every year of error adds one point. A perfect answer has zero error. The direction of the mistake has no effect on the number, and large misses remain visible instead of being capped at 100.

Your guess 1900Correct year 2000Year error 100 pts
  • 0 Excellent · celebration
  • 1–10 Very nice · two thumbs up
  • 11–30 Good · thumbs up
  • 31–50 Average · a shrug
  • 51+ Poor · a disappointed Fern

A timed-out question adds 1000 points. At the end, your seven scores add together above the results list. Fern’s final reaction uses your average score per question. Each row keeps the correct year and your guess available in smaller text.

Eraly Daily and Practice modes

Daily challenge

You have 25 seconds for each of seven events. The next clock begins when you advance from a revealed answer. Use the pause between records to read the note, then continue when ready.

Untimed practice

Take longer to reason through a clue. Practice draws from the same 35-record collection, so repeats are possible. It is a useful way to become familiar with the interface and the scoring before trying a daily round.

Eraly tips: build a timeline in your head.

Start with an event you can place confidently. Ask whether the clue belongs before or after it, then narrow the interval. A spacecraft launch, for example, can be compared with the first satellite or the first Moon landing. After the reveal, remember the relationship between the events as well as the isolated date.

The game currently covers common-era events from 1066 to 2021. You can enter years from 1 to 9999. The answer bank is finite, and remembering a previously revealed date is part of practice.

Eraly game questions

How do I play Eraly?

Choose Daily for seven timed historical events or Practice for an untimed set. Enter the year of each event, submit your guess, and check the correct date after the reveal. Complete all seven records to see your total error.

Is this the official Eraly game?

This is an independent Eraly edition on Farflung Noillirk. It uses its own questions, local progress and scoring. Daily sets and results belong to this edition.

Can I play Eraly free without signing in?

Yes. Both modes on this page run in your browser without a game account or payment. Daily progress can be saved on this device when browser storage is available.

Where can I see today’s Eraly answers?

This edition reveals each correct year after you submit an answer or the timer expires. Its final ledger lists all seven dates. Those answers apply to the daily set played here.

What does a score of 100 mean?

Your answer was 100 years away from the event. A guess of 1900 for an event in 2000 earns 100 points. Earlier and later guesses are treated equally.

When does the daily set change?

A new set is selected for each UTC calendar date. Everyone playing this edition gets the same seven records for that date. Practice chooses another set from the finite bank, so familiar questions can return.

Why did Fern give my final result a different reaction?

The final reaction uses the average error across seven questions. Your total remains visible above the ledger. Seven answers that each miss by ten years produce 70 total points and a Very nice reaction.

Can I return to an unfinished round?

Daily answers are saved in this browser when local storage is available. A timer already running continues while you are away. Pause between revealed records if you need a break. Practice begins a fresh run.

Can I compare my score with a friend?

Share the daily result after completing all seven records. Compare the same UTC date and edition. Practice sets can differ, so their totals do not measure performance on identical questions.
