Know your score.

Rare Dive has its own question bank and editorial scoring. Daily uses a fixed UTC-day set; Practice shuffles a finite bank. Scores are separate from Globe Crowd, Popular Answers and original Krillion.

Read the source game’s guide ↗

Looking for the original?

This page embeds Rare Dive, an independent Krillion-inspired edition hosted on noillirk.co. For the original experience, visit Krillion. No scores or accounts are shared with that service.

If the game does not appear

Try reloading or opening it in a new tab. Browser privacy settings, network availability and the game service can affect embeds. The hub cannot confirm a completed game from an iframe load event. On phones, the independent page gives the game more room.

What is Krillion?

Krillion is known for rare-answer trivia and the daily dive theme: think of something valid that others may overlook. The embedded Rare Dive edition uses its own prompts and editorial scoring, rather than the original Krillion answer sheet or leaderboard.

A useful way to approach each question

Find the category’s boundaries first, then think beyond its most famous examples. A less familiar answer still needs to be correct and accepted by this edition’s bank. Review the result to learn which answers were recognized.

Krillion questions, answered

Does this edition have Daily and Practice?

Rare Dive offers a fixed UTC-day set in Daily and a shuffled finite bank in Practice. Repeats are possible; Practice does not mean an unlimited supply of unique questions.

Can I use original Krillion answers here?

The question banks are separate. An answer or hint for the original daily dive may not fit this edition’s prompts. This site does not publish a verified original daily answer sheet.

Are scores comparable with Noillirk or Farflung?

No. The games have separate scoring rules, and original-service scores are separate too. There is no combined leaderboard here.
