Rare Dive questions, scoring and progress

Question bank

Rare Dive checks answers against its own curated bank. If an answer is missing from the list, try another that fits the clue. Practice draws from a finite pool, so questions can repeat.

Scoring

Listed answers have editor-assigned rarity scores based on how obvious they are. You can earn up to 100 points per round, with a maximum of 700 points per dive. Each point takes you 10 metres deeper.

Progress

When browser storage is available, Rare Dive remembers the prompts you’ve seen and your best completed score. Saves stay in the browser, and private browsing or clearing site data can remove them. Leaving a dive before the end means starting a new one when you return.

If the game does not appear

Try reloading or opening it in a new tab. Browser privacy settings, network availability and the game service can affect embeds. The hub cannot confirm a completed game from an iframe load event. On phones, the independent page gives the game more room.

What is Rare Dive?

Rare Dive is a seven-round trivia game you can play free in your browser. Find an answer that fits the clue and earns enough points to take you deeper.

A useful way to approach each question

Find the category’s boundaries first, then think beyond its most famous examples. A less familiar answer still needs to be correct and accepted by this edition’s bank. Review the result to learn which answers were recognized.

Krillion questions, answered

Does this edition have Daily and Practice?

Rare Dive offers a fixed UTC-day set in Daily and a shuffled finite bank in Practice. Repeats are possible; Practice does not mean an unlimited supply of unique questions.

Why was my answer rejected?

Rare Dive only scores answers found in its own list. Check your spelling or try another answer that fits the clue.

Are scores comparable with Noillirk or Farflung?

Each game has its own scoring rules and keeps its results separate.
