FARFLUNG NOILLIRK / THE FIELD NOTES

Privacy Policy

Scope and contact

Updated November 3, 2026. This policy covers farflungnoillirk.com. Contact marlonqiu2027@gmail.com with privacy questions or deletion requests concerning information you sent us.

Local preferences

Eraly stores daily guesses and progress in this browser, including the active question deadline. Clearing browser storage removes that progress. The hub stores favorite game IDs and the three most recently opened game-page IDs in your browser. Favorites and recent visits are separate from the local Eraly save. These records are not synced to an account. Browser data removal can delete them; browser restrictions can prevent storage.

Search and accounts

Game search runs locally against the game catalog. We do not send search text to a search service. Registration and login are not yet open, and this version does not collect passwords or create accounts.

Embedded games

Eraly loads from this site. Loading one of the other embedded games connects to noillirk.co. That origin receives the requests needed to serve the game and may use its own storage. Its practices and game progress are separate from the hub. Opening original-game links connects to third-party services governed by their own policies.

Traffic measurement

The hub loads a Plausible-compatible analytics script from data.1back.link/js/app.js and sends traffic measurement requests to data.1back.link/api/send for the domain farflungnoillirk.com. We do not deliberately attach email addresses, answers, passwords or search text to analytics. Exact server-side retention and operator arrangements must be confirmed before launch; we do not claim zero collection.

Installation and offline behavior

The installed hub can show a minimal offline message. External iframe games are not cached for offline play. Installation does not synchronize game saves.

Messages and requests

If you email us, your email provider and ours process that correspondence. Share only what is necessary. Ask us to review or delete messages you supplied; legal and operational obligations may affect what can be removed. Hosting logs and analytics retention require deployment-stage verification.
