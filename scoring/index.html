FARFLUNG NOILLIRK / THE FIELD NOTES

Scoring Explained

GD Krillion

The external player handles Rare and Crowd scoring.

Medical Krillion

Rare favours less common accepted answers. Crowd favours more common accepted answers. Live scoring is provided by the external game.

Farflung

Five clues per flight. Popular and Rare modes use editor-estimated country ranks, not a live player survey. The question bank is a preview and still needs human review.

Noillirk

The development edition uses 21 test questions and simulated weights. Seven timed questions use fixed score bands; these are not real crowd percentages. The public API reports a test-only Cloudflare preview with multiplayer and authentication disabled. Full-round play has not been browser-verified.

Krillion

Rare Dive has its own question bank and editorial scoring. Daily uses a fixed UTC-day set; Practice shuffles a finite bank. Scores are separate from Globe Crowd, Popular Answers and original Krillion.

Eraly

One point for each year away from the answer. Zero is perfect. A missed daily question adds 1000 points. The final character reaction uses average error across seven records.
