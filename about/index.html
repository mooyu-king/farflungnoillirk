FARFLUNG NOILLIRK / THE FIELD NOTES

About Us

A small collection for curious minds

Farflung Noillirk brings together three independent games hosted on noillirk.co: Globe Crowd, Popular Answers and Rare Dive. Our purpose is to make their different styles easy to discover and understand.

Independent editions, clear boundaries

This site is not the official Farflung, Noillirk or Krillion service and does not claim endorsement from their creators. Each edition has separate questions, scoring and progress. Game pages provide links to the corresponding original services.

Preview means preview

We disclose editorial scoring and test data rather than presenting them as player surveys. Public API availability, embedded play and real-device behavior still need verification before a full release.

Help improve the collection

Send broken links, accessibility issues and content corrections to mooyuking@gmail.com. Please identify the game and describe the issue without sending passwords or private information.
